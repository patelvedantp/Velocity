# 90-second video tutorial

Before recording: load the real match, close unrelated tabs, and enable your screen recorder's microphone. Actions in brackets are directions, not narration. Read at a conversational pace.

## 0:00–0:12
[Show the real match title and pitch.]
“This is Touchline, a 3D soccer tracking studio. Here, I’ve loaded SkillCorner’s Melbourne Victory versus Auckland match. The markers show the players, and the yellow highlight identifies the ball.”

## 0:12–0:25
[Click Play, Pause, drag the timeline, and choose 0.5× speed.]
“Press Play to follow the action. Pause whenever you need, or drag this timeline to jump to a moment. Playback speed lets you slow down a sequence for a closer look.”

## 0:25–0:39
[Click Free orbit and drag; choose a player, then Player follow.]
“Tactical shows the pitch from above. Free Orbit lets you rotate and zoom. Select a player and choose Player Follow to keep that player in view. Trails show recent movement.”

## 0:39–0:57
[Pause. Click Set start at playhead. Set the end seven seconds later. Name the clip and select Transition.]
“To make a clip, set the start at the playhead and choose an end within ten seconds. Give it a name and select a tag. The I and O shortcuts also set the boundaries when you’re outside an editing field.”

## 0:57–1:10
[Click Preview, then Save. Point to the colored badge.]
“Preview checks the selected sequence. Save adds it to the library with its tag. Clips are stored locally by match, so reloading the same match restores them in this browser.”

## 1:10–1:30
[Open Share / export and click Download portable JSON clip. Point to the WebM option and timestamp field.]
“Share and Export offers a portable clip that another user can import without the full match. WebM records the current 3D view. Timestamp links require an accessible app and the same match loaded. Recording quality depends on the browser and hardware.”
